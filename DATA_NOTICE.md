# Data notice

Original filings come from [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/).
The [acquisition guide](docs/dataset.md#acquisition-and-source-storage) explains how to download and import filings for this repository.

This notice describes the local source collection as inspected on 2026-09-25.
The source manifest retains the source URLs and original names.

The initial collection contained 60 files, including four incomplete downloads.
The complete candidates comprised 53 filing documents and three SEC master indexes before retention review.
The incomplete files are excluded from the retained raw collection.
An initial email-pattern scan found candidates in 18 files.
That scan does not establish privacy compliance or the absence of sensitive content.

Third-party rights remain separate from the project's code license.
The [release guide](docs/release.md) identifies two generator notices that require individual review.
Do not remove notices, claim ownership of third-party material, or apply a blanket CC0 dedication.
The SEC does not endorse this project.

View downloaded HTML as text or through the sandboxed source review tool.
Opening raw HTML directly in a browser can execute active content.
Do not serve raw files through GitHub Pages or an authenticated application.
Preserve original bytes when preparing safe previews.

Before a project archive release, follow the [release guide](docs/release.md).
Record the approved snapshot, manifest, source attribution, and known omissions in this notice.
Use a project issue for nonsensitive corrections or private platform reporting for sensitive material.
Do not publish private details in an issue.
Corrections cannot guarantee removal from copies that others downloaded.
